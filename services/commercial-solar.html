Commercial Solar

Team V provides electrical and construction support for commercial solar across rooftop, canopy, and ground mount systems. We handle balance of system work, supporting infrastructure, and interconnection so solar can supplement grid output and integrate cleanly with storage and EV charging.

Project Experience

Cubix / Domain Carports

Solar carport canopies

Ground-mount solar array

Ground-mount solar installation as part of a larger hybrid energy project. Structured for efficient construction and straightforward interconnection with storage and site loads.
